# The shells

Pictures of the playground's devices from the front, served under `/shells/`.
`bun tools/handheld-shells.ts microts` renders the ones in this directory in
Blender (`tools/handheld-models/shells.py`) and writes `profiles.js`; the PSP
and the 3DS are the Pocket3D player's
(`devices/web/pocket-web-wgpu/web/shells`). No picture carries a wordmark or a
logo: the pieces that are one are taken out of the model, or painted over in
its picture.

Changes to the models of other authors for these pictures:

- **Game Boy Advance**: the case is recoloured from the model's silver to
  indigo; the maker's name over the screen is taken out (the badge keeps its
  rim and gets a floor in the case's colour), and the console's name under the
  screen is painted over in the lens's colour. The model's screen outline
  gives way to a 3:2 panel, and the power lamp is lit. The keys are rendered
  apart from the case.
- **iPhone 4S**: the sheet of front glass is left out and the black under it
  is given a polish; the lock screen gives way to a dark panel; the home
  button's picture gives way to a dark dish with a square; the camera's glass
  is dark.
- **iPhone 5**, shown as an iPod touch of the fifth generation: the earpiece
  and the sensor beside it are taken out and the opening is closed in the
  face's white; the screen gives way to a dark panel.
- **BlackBerry Classic**: the maker's mark on the Menu key is painted over
  with three bars; the home screen gives way to a dark panel; Space and Enter
  are rendered apart from the case.
- **PSP**: the PSP wordmark, the PlayStation logo and the Memory Stick mark
  are left out; the keys, the nub and the shoulder keys are rendered apart from
  the case; the materials have finishes the file does not carry.
